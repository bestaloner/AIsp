The Challenge / 行业痛点

Construction Safety Can't Rely on Human Eyes Alone

Traditional safety inspection is labor-intensive, inconsistent, and reactive. Sites are too large, hazards change too fast, and inspectors can't be everywhere at once.

传统安全巡检依赖人力巡查，覆盖面有限、标准不统一、响应滞后。工地面积大、隐患变化快，检查员无法同时覆盖所有点位。

🔍

Limited Coverage / 覆盖盲区

Manual patrols can only inspect a fraction of the site at any given moment. Critical hazards in blind spots go undetected for hours or days.

⏰

Delayed Response / 响应滞后

Hours pass between hazard occurrence and human discovery. By the time an inspector arrives, the situation may have already escalated into an incident.

📋

Inconsistent Judgment / 标准不一

Different inspectors have different standards. What one flags as dangerous, another may overlook — leading to unpredictable safety outcomes.

🔄

Broken Follow-Up / 整改断链

Hazards are found but rectification isn't tracked. No closed-loop system means issues are reported but never verified as resolved. Accountability is lost.

Our Solution / 解决方案

AI Eyes That Never Blink. Human Judgment That Never Falters.

VisionGuard combines YOLO real-time object detection with VLM (Vision Language Model) secondary verification — AI flags every potential hazard, human reviewers confirm or dismiss, and the platform tracks every issue through to resolution.

VisionGuard 融合YOLO实时目标检测与VLM视觉大模型二次复核——AI全量初筛，人工精准确认，平台全程追踪每一处隐患直到整改闭环。
